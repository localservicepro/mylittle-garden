Hedge Trimming · Yarra Valley

A well-kept hedge frames a garden beautifully and gives you privacy where it matters. Our hedge trimming in the Yarra Valley keeps your hedges sharp, even and thriving.

From formal box hedges to tall screening plants around Healesville, Yarra Glen and the surrounding suburbs, we shape with care and always clean up the clippings afterwards.

What's included

Trimming that keeps hedges thick and green

Regular trimming does more than tidy — it encourages hedges to grow denser and healthier from the inside out, giving you a fuller screen and a cleaner line.

We time and shape each cut to suit the plant, so your hedges look their best without stress or bare patches.

  • Shaping and trimming of formal and informal hedges
  • Height and width reduction to keep hedges in bounds
  • Trimming of screening and privacy hedges
  • Encouraging dense, healthy new growth
  • Full clean-up and removal of clippings
