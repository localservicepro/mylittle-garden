Brushcutting · Yarra Valley

When a lawn mower simply won't cut it, our brushcutting in the Yarra Valley is the answer. We tackle thick, overgrown grass, weeds and rough terrain that regular mowing can't handle.

Taming overgrown gardens is our specialty. Whether you've bought a neglected property, fallen behind over a busy season, or need a block cleared for sale or fire preparation, we'll cut it back and leave it neat and manageable.

What's included

From jungle to tidy in a single visit

Overgrown grass doesn't just look untidy — it harbours pests, increases fire risk and can breach council requirements. Our commercial brushcutters make short work of even the heaviest growth.

Once the block is cut back, we can set you up on a regular mowing or maintenance schedule so it never gets out of hand again.

  • Cutting back of long grass, weeds and light scrub
  • Clearing of rough edges, verges and hard-to-reach areas
  • Reclaiming neglected and overgrown blocks
  • Fire-season and pre-sale block tidy-ups
  • Full clean-up and removal of cut material on request
