Lawn Mowing & Edging · Yarra Valley

A freshly mown lawn is the fastest way to lift the look of any property. My Little Garden Helper provides dependable lawn mowing in the Yarra Valley and across Eastern Melbourne, keeping your grass neat, green and healthy all season long.

From compact suburban lawns in Lilydale to sprawling rural blocks around Three Bridges and Healesville, we bring the right mowers and a keen eye for detail to every visit — and we always tidy up before we leave.

What's included

Regular mowing that keeps your lawn thriving

Mowing at the right height and frequency is one of the best things you can do for lawn health. Cut too short or too rarely and grass thins out, weeds move in and bare patches appear.

We tailor a schedule to your lawn type and the season — usually every 1–2 weeks through the warmer months and less often in winter — so your grass stays thick, green and resilient year round.

  • Mowing of front, back and side lawns to a healthy, even height
  • Clean edging along paths, driveways, garden beds and fence lines
  • Line trimming around obstacles, trees and tight corners
  • Clippings collected or mulched to your preference
  • Blow-down of paths and hard surfaces so everything looks finished
