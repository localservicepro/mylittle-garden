Mulching · Yarra Valley

Mulch is one of the best investments you can make in your garden. Our mulching in the Yarra Valley protects your soil, cuts down watering and keeps weeds at bay.

We supply and spread quality mulch across garden beds and around trees and shrubs throughout the Yarra Valley and Eastern Melbourne, leaving your garden looking rich, tidy and well cared for.

What's included

Less watering, fewer weeds, healthier plants

A good layer of mulch keeps soil cool and moist through our warm Yarra Valley summers, meaning less watering and stronger, healthier plants.

It also smothers weeds before they start and slowly feeds your soil as it breaks down — so your beds stay lower-maintenance and better-looking for longer.

  • Supply and spreading of quality garden mulch
  • Even coverage across beds, borders and around trees
  • Weed suppression and moisture retention
  • Neat, defined bed edges for a finished look
  • Bed preparation and light weeding before mulching
